Scientific notes & sources

Myopia and hyperopia use a simplified thin-eye model with limited accommodation. Ray displacement is enlarged for teaching. Vision effects illustrate concepts and do not reproduce a particular person's vision. Hyperopia here means refractive long-sightedness, rather than age-related presbyopia. Glaucoma can occur at normal eye pressure; early disease may have no symptoms. Treatment does not restore lost vision. Cataract replacement shows an idealized outcome.
